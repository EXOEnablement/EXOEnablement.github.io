Is your SDR team a fit for fractional enablement? Score it straight.

Nine questions, two scores — because fit and timing are not the same thing. Fit (out of 70): function, team reality and authority — could EXO actually help this team? Urgency (out of 30): timing signals and pain — is now the moment? No email required. No gloss on the result.

Function fit — 30 points

1. What does your outbound motion look like?

2. Who owns the SDR function day to day?

Team reality — 25 points

3. How many SDRs are in the team today?

4. Is there a manager in place for the team?

Authority — 15 points

5. If this scored well, who are you in the decision?

Timing signals — 20 points

6. Hiring in the next six months?

7. Any change event on the horizon?

Pain evidence — 10 points

8. Ramp and attrition — how real is the pain?

9. How consistent is coaching across the team?
