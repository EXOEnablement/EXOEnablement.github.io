How this portal works

Each person has one active focus skill at a time. Every coaching session is tied to that focus, produces tasks, and is recorded under a privacy tier: shared (coach + coachee), coachee-only journal, or your private notes. Managers see aggregate progress in the Buyer Report — never private notes or journal content.

Between sessions, use the prompt on each person's card to set a short reflection task. Responses come back as journal entries at the next session.
